RAM Limiter: how to install (macOS 14 or newer)

1. Drag "RAM Limiter" (the app) into your Applications folder.
2. Put the "extension" folder somewhere it can stay, such as Documents.
   Your browser keeps using it from there, so don't delete or move it.
3. Open RAM Limiter from Applications. macOS blocks it the first time,
   because the app isn't notarized by Apple. Click Done (not Move to Bin).
4. Open System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, click
   Open Anyway next to RAM Limiter, and confirm. You only do this once.
5. In your browser, open the extensions page:
   opera://extensions (also Opera GX)   chrome://extensions   edge://extensions
   brave://extensions   vivaldi://extensions
6. Turn on Developer mode.
7. Click Load unpacked and choose the "extension" folder.
8. Click the purple RAM Limiter icon in the menu bar. Within about a minute
   it should say "extension: connected".
9. Refresh tabs that were already open.

The limiter starts ON with a 4 GB limit.
Protection isn't guaranteed: tick important sites under Never unload.
