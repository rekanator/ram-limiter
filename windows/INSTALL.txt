RAM Limiter: how to install (Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit)

1. Unzip the download (right-click it > Extract All). Put the "RAM Limiter"
   folder somewhere it can stay, such as Documents. Windows and your browser
   keep using these files, so don't delete or move them.
2. Open RAMLimiter.exe. Windows may say "Windows protected your PC", because
   the app isn't signed. Click More info, then Run anyway.
   If Smart App Control is on, Windows blocks the app and offers no way to
   run it.
3. The RAM Limiter icon appears near the clock. It may be hidden under the
   ^ arrow; drag it onto the taskbar to keep it visible.
   No icon and no ^ arrow? Right-click an empty part of the taskbar, choose
   Taskbar settings, open Other system tray icons and switch RAMLimiter on.
4. In your browser, open the extensions page:
   opera://extensions (also Opera GX)   chrome://extensions   edge://extensions
   brave://extensions   vivaldi://extensions
5. Turn on Developer mode.
6. Click Load unpacked and choose the "extension" folder.
7. Click the RAM Limiter icon near the clock. Within about a minute it should
   say "extension: connected".
8. Refresh tabs that were already open.

The limiter starts ON with a 4 GB limit.
Protection isn't guaranteed: tick important sites under Never unload.
